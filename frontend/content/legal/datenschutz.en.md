## 3. User account

To register we process your name, e-mail address, password (stored only as a secure hash), the chosen role (learner or instructor), your preferred language and the time you accepted the terms of use. You may optionally add a profile picture and a short bio. Legal basis: performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Instructor accounts are reviewed by us before they are activated; we see the instructor's profile data for this purpose.

The instructor of a course sees this data for their own course so they can teach and grade you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

We use **no** tracking, analytics or advertising cookies and no analytics services, so there is no cookie banner. We only use what is technically necessary (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG):

- `refresh_token`: http-only cookie that keeps you signed in (up to 14 days, removed when you sign out).
- `NEXT_LOCALE`: remembers the chosen language (1 year).
- Your browser's local storage for interface preferences such as light/dark mode and collapsed sidebars, a backup of quiz answers not yet submitted, the page to return to after signing in and a hint whether a session exists. This data never leaves your device.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

You can do much of this yourself: under **Settings → Privacy & data** you can download all your data as a ZIP file and delete your account. For anything else, write to {{PLACEHOLDER: privacy contact e-mail}}.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. {{PLACEHOLDER: competent state data protection authority at the operator's seat}}.

## 10. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. File links are signed and expire.
